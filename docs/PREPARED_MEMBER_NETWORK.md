# Experimental Member network adapter

The private `_prepared_member_network` module consumes ANYgeometry's
`PreparedMemberSheetJointNetwork` query/validation receipt. It does not change
the accepted finite `_prepared_member_accounting` module, any public meshing
route, the 7f engine runner, quality policies or execution gates.

The candidate owner is commit `4dd5c51c4b9d31863b275bc1234b6387435e9896`;
wheel SHA256 `530b82d493efc8bdcbf42f39bc35c374987891b18a1e7079a5b5f15ff3ec992c`.
Owner receipt qualification covers connected Plane/Straight Sheet documents,
continuous straight boundary Member chains with distinct original carriers,
and exact edge-local point references. Unsupported carriers and repeated
original carriers remain owner refusals.

Accounting retains every original/current structural record, Member traversal,
original-use identity, native interval certificate and point relation. Native
persisted Member intervals and attachment parameters are preserved as exact
rationals of their stored values. Polynomial ancestry is retained separately;
its ideal ancestor stations do not replace native parameters. Owner and
constraint receipts undergo full fresh binding checks, not digest-only approval.
Evidence is pinned before callbacks and after final owner checks.

Independent review reproduced a request-binding defect in the initial candidate:
a valid station proof for another edge could supply the requested edge's rows.
The corrected adapter requires the exact public `AuthoredBoundaryStations` type,
the requested integer edge ID and the complete requested owner correspondence
before consuming coordinates. It pins every proof field, including correspondence,
UV and tolerance, across all later callbacks and closing validation. Regressions
also substitute a valid proof for the same edge on a different owner occurrence.
Missing or unusable station capabilities produce typed `MeshError` refusals.
The reproduced failure and the initial malformed regression fixture are retained;
the repaired candidate requires review against its new source identity.

Station planning shares a single row per edge/local station across all Member
uses, using current polynomial coordinates and authoritative boundary queries.
All available owner boundary occurrences must agree on those coordinates.
Member stations follow each retained use's orientation and global interval;
point intents retain their separate source vertex identities even at endpoints.
No coordinate matching, node welding or beam construction is performed. Explicit
station budgets are retained and may only decrease during validation; this is a
request resource bound, not an owner model-count cap.

Focused geometry-only evidence is under
`reports/authored-root/member-network-adapter-4dd5c51/`. Initial failures are
retained alongside corrected checks. The disposable owner fixture covers 3–10
faces, not a kernel limit. Independent adapter review remains required. No
100/1000 mixed-mesh, quadratic, solver, publication or release claim is made.
Every mesh, beam, solver and publication flag remains false. Adding this module
changes the live mesher source composition; historical frozen proposals remain
immutable and must not be dispatched against changed composition.
